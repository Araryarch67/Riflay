# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Riflay's issues, discussions and pull requests to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their age,
body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Good:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to open source
- Respecting different opinions, setups and ways of listening to music
- Giving and taking constructive feedback gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all of the project's spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat linked from the
README) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[a private report on GitHub](https://github.com/Araryarch67/Riflay/security/advisories/new) (choose it even when it
isn't a security issue, it reaches the maintainer only) or by contacting the maintainer on their GitHub profile.
Every report is reviewed, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow
this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.
